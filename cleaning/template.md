# Cleaned voice-log template

Structure the cleaned body with the sections below. Omit any section that has
no content — do not pad. Do not include a top-level `#` title (the tool adds it).

## Context
What was being worked on, and why. One short paragraph.

## What happened
The substance: observations, what was tried, what was found. Bullet points or
short paragraphs. Keep the speaker's first-person voice.

## Issues / blockers
Anything that went wrong or is still open. Omit if none were mentioned.

## Decisions
Choices that were made out loud (e.g. "we'll use whisper.cpp"). Omit if none.

## Next steps
- Concrete follow-ups the speaker named. One per line. Omit the section if none
  were stated — do not invent next steps.
